1. High-probability discovery candidate: **N179 (continuous biological support score interaction)**.

- Promotion gate: significant positive geometry-support interaction and top-decile support uplift in at least two domains.

- Promotion gate: out-of-sample `delta R^2` significantly above permutation null in at least two domain-splits.
