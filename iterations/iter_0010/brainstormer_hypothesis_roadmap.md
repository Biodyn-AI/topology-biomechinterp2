- Decision gate: require retained geodesic lift over Euclidean after transfer and non-trivial neighborhood overlap.

- Why now: low-cost mechanistic discriminator that can quickly validate whether local nonlinearity drives manifold gains.
- Decision gate: require consistent positive slope across both disjoint splits with permutation significance.
